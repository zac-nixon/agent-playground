# Write another poem about kubecon

The badges are printed, the lanyards are worn,
the keynote hall fills with a communal yawn
that melts into wonder when demos ignite
and CNCF banners blaze blue through the light.

A thousand containers set sail in the air,
Helmcharts and operators drifting out there,
the hallway track hums with the gossip of ops:
"We migrated off Nomad" — the conversation stops.

There are talks about scaling and talks about mesh,
about chasing the tail of whatever is fresh,
abot eBPF wizards who conjure the kernel,
and SREs dreaming of sleep that's eternal.

The expo floor gleams with its plush-toy menagerie,
a kraken, a gopher, a shark from the registry,
swag bags grow heavy with stickers and pens,
and everyone swears they'll come back again.

For somewhere in all of the chaos and hype,
between every demo and every last gripe,
we find what we came for: the people, the craft,
the shared understanding of what we have built and what's next on the raft.

_Requested in #42._
