# Write another poem about kubecon

The badges swing on lanyards in a hall
where clusters hum and keynotes fill the air,
a thousand engineers who heed the call
of containers, cold brew, and t-shirts rare.

The YAML flows like water, line by line,
operators deployed with practiced care,
and someone's Helm chart, polished, almost fine,
has broken prod—but no one will declare.

We gather here from cities, coasts, and cloud,
to argue over namespaces and nodes,
to sketch on napkins, speaking fast and loud
of service meshes, GitOps, and the odes

we write in terminals at 2 a.m.
KubeCon ends. We'll do it all again.

_Requested in #44._
