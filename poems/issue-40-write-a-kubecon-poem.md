# Write a kubecon poem

A thousand engineers converge on the hall,
where containers hum and clusters stand tall,
the badges swing wide on their colorful strings,
and every new keynote brings bold, hopeful things.

The booths bloom like gardens of YAML and code,
operators pitching their manifests bold,
Helm charts unfurled like bright flags in the air,
and Prometheus watching the metrics with care.

In corridors humming with coffee and talk,
a SRE pauses mid-hallway to walk
through a problem she traced to a node that won't drain—
then laughs when she finds it's a label to blame.

We schedule our pods and we schedule our days,
we gossip in liveness-probe, readiness-haze,
we open-source dreamers in hoodies and hats,
debating the merits of this versus that.

But here in this city of clouds spun from wire,
we're drawn by a common, insatiable fire:
to build the kind of world where systems hold true,
where scale is a feature, and nothing is glue.

So raise up your kubeconfig, lift your CLI,
to the clusters we tend under some distant sky—
KubeCon reminds us, through talk, demo, and cheer,
that the future runs Kubernetes, and we brought it here.

_Requested in #40._
