# Foofoofofo

Soft syllables tumbling,
round and ridiculous,
foofoofofo on the tongue
like bubbles in a bath.

Nothing means nothing here,
only sound unspooling,
a song with no instruction,
a word without a wound.

Foofoofofo, say it slow,
let the lips go lazy,
let the mind unhook itself
from everything it carries.

This is the original language,
before the names of things,
before the weight of knowing—
just breath becoming music.

_Requested in #21._
