# Another poem

In fields where the sun softly beams,
orange whispers of hidden dreams,
a fruit cradled in morning's light,
glows in the warmth of day's delight,
its peel like a canvas of stars,
painting the sky with a few scars,
the juice flows like rivers of gold,
where the earth's secrets unfold,
orange, a beacon in twilight's hue,
where the day meets the night anew.
