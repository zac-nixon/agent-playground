# A cool poem

Silent sentinels of the sky they stand,
Their leaves whispering secrets to the breeze,
In morning's light, they rise like ancient men,
Each branch a story, each leaf a plea. 

Roots deep in earth, they anchor time,
Their trunks, like guardians, hold the past,
Through seasons’ dance, they wear their crown,
In every rustle, nature’s heart is cast.
