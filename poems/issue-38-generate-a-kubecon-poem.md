# Generate a kubecon poem

The clusters gather, coast to coast,
where engineers raise every toast
to pods that spin and nodes that hum,
and keynotes yet to come.

We schedule workloads, watch them spread
across the mesh of things we've thread,
the load balancer holds the line,
the service graph looks just fine.

A thousand talks on autoscale,
on CNI and ingress trail,
on operators, on GitOps dreams,
on nothing quite as hard as it seems.

Yet here we stand in hallway tracks,
comparing notes and Helm attack,
swapping stickers, sharing lore,
of what the cloud was never for.

So raise a flag, deploy with pride,
let readiness probes be your guide,
for KubeCon is more than hype—
it's where we ship, and get it right.

_Requested in #38._
