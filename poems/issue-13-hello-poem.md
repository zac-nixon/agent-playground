# Hello Poem

Hello, hello, the word rings out,
A simple sound without a doubt,
It travels far on morning air,
A greeting tossed without a care,
Hello to strangers on the street,
Hello to everyone you meet,
Hello to sunshine, hello to rain,
Hello to joy and hello to pain,
Hello to mountains standing tall,
Hello to rivers, hello to all,
Hello to oceans deep and wide,
Hello to the changing of the tide,
Hello to forests dark and green,
Hello to every sight and scene,
Hello to children as they play,
Hello to the breaking of the day,
Hello to stars that light the night,
Hello to darkness, hello to light,
Hello to seasons as they turn,
Hello to lessons that we learn,
Hello to winter cold and grey,
Hello to spring that comes to stay,
Hello to summer burning bright,
Hello to autumn's golden light,
Hello to laughter, hello to song,
Hello to right and hello to wrong,
Hello to memories of old,
Hello to stories yet untold,
Hello to places far away,
Hello to those who cannot stay,
Hello to dreamers and their dreams,
Hello to rivers, lakes and streams,
Hello to silence deep and still,
Hello to the whisper of the hill,
Hello to breezes soft and light,
Hello to the coming of the night,
Hello to the moon that climbs so high,
Hello to clouds that drift on by,
Hello to birds upon the wing,
Hello to the songs that we all sing,
Hello to hope when days are long,
Hello to the melody of song,
Hello to every open door,
Hello to hearts that ask for more,
Hello to kindness, hello to grace,
Hello to every smiling face,
Hello to choices that we make,
Hello to the paths we choose to take,
Hello to bridges over streams,
Hello to the fabric of our dreams,
Hello to farmers in their fields,
Hello to all the harvest yields,
Hello to teachers and their care,
Hello to knowledge that we share,
Hello to doctors healing hands,
Hello to those who understand,
Hello to builders raising walls,
Hello to the voice that calls,
Hello to the quiet of the dawn,
Hello to the dew upon the lawn,
Hello to the sparrows in the tree,
Hello to the buzzing of the bee,
Hello to the butterfly in flight,
Hello to the fireflies at night,
Hello to the ocean's crashing wave,
Hello to the hero and the brave,
Hello to the shy and soft spoken,
Hello to the hearts that have been broken,
Hello to the mending that takes place,
Hello to the smile upon your face,
Hello to the roads that stretch ahead,
Hello to the words that go unsaid,
Hello to the letters in the mail,
Hello to the story and the tale,
Hello to the artist and their brush,
Hello to the morning's gentle hush,
Hello to the baker and their bread,
Hello to the thoughts inside your head,
Hello to the runner in their stride,
Hello to the feeling deep inside,
Hello to the garden and its bloom,
Hello to the flowers in the room,
Hello to the candle burning bright,
Hello to its warm and gentle light,
Hello to the book upon the shelf,
Hello to the time we spend ourself,
Hello to the puzzle and its pieces,
Hello to the moment that never ceases,
Hello to the laughter of a child,
Hello to the free and to the wild,
Hello to the tame and to the calm,
Hello to the healing and the balm,
Hello to the sailor on the sea,
Hello to the wind that blows so free,
Hello to the anchor and the shore,
Hello to the knock upon the door,
Hello to the welcoming inside,
Hello to the warmth you cannot hide,
Hello to the table set with care,
Hello to the food and those who share,
Hello to the glasses raised up high,
Hello to the years that flutter by,
Hello to the wrinkles on the face,
Hello to the wisdom and the grace,
Hello to the elder and the young,
Hello to the songs that have been sung,
Hello to the babies newly born,
Hello to the bright and to the worn,
Hello to the faithful and the true,
Hello to the skies of brilliant blue,
Hello to the grey and overcast,
Hello to the future and the past,
Hello to the present moment here,
Hello to everything we hold so dear,
Hello to the love that fills the heart,
Hello to the ending and the start,
Hello to the final setting sun,
Hello to the day that has been done,
Hello to the night that wraps us tight,
Hello to tomorrow's promised light,
Hello to the world in all its ways,
Hello to the end of all our days,
Hello to forever and beyond,
Hello to the ties of which we're fond,
Hello to the bond that holds us near,
Hello to the voices that we hear,
Hello to the echo in the hall,
Hello, hello, hello to all.
