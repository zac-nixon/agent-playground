# A third poem

In the twilight's embrace, where shadows softly creep,
Whispers of an ancient tale, through time's silent keep,
A journey unfolds, where secrets softly sleep,
Beneath the sky's deep sigh, in silence it seeps.

A traveler treads with grace, through valleys wide and deep,
Each step a sonnet, each breath a gentle sweep,
Of winds that weave through time, in endless sleep,
To where the world's heartbeats, in silence, gently weep.

The ancient forest stands, a sentinel so wise,
Its towering limbs stretch high, beneath the moon's pale eyes,
A tapestry of leaves, in twilight's soft disguise,
Each rustle tells of secrets, in nature's quiet cries.

In the hush of night, where stars like diamonds gleam,
The traveler pauses, to listen to the ancient stream,
Its murmurs speak of worlds, where time is but a dream,
Of lands unseen, where souls in twilight softly gleam.

Beneath the starlit canopy, where dreams take flight,
The traveler sits in quiet, beneath the starlit night,
The universe expands, in silent, endless might,
Each twinkling star a beacon, in the cosmic light.

In this sacred space, where silence holds its breath,
The traveler finds a truth, in nature's quiet depth,
A whisper of eternity, in every gentle sweep,
Of winds that dance through time, in silence they seep.

Through valleys deep and wide, where secrets softly sleep,
