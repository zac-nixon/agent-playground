# Make me a poem about kube con

Thousands of engineers descend on the hall,
badges and backpacks and laptops for all,
the hum of a cluster runs under the floor,
and someone is live-demoing Helm once more.

The pods keep restarting, but nobody minds,
a world of containers and curious minds,
operators, sidecars, and service mesh dreams,
chaos on purpose — or so the talk seems.

A keynote erupts with the flash of a slide,
"cloud-native future" announced far and wide,
the CNCF banner hangs proud in the air,
and sticker collections beyond all compare.

We argue about namespaces over our drinks,
debate what a healthy liveness probe thinks,
then stumble on talks about eBPF nights,
and watch someone scale to a million delights.

So here's to KubeCon, the nerdiest fest,
where YAML is poetry, chaos is blessed,
where every pull request is somebody's art,
and Kubernetes beats at the cloud-native heart.
